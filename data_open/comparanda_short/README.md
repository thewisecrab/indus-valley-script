# Short-text comparanda

`wiki_titles_<lang>.txt`: 6,000 Wikipedia article titles per language (fi, ta, sa), one per line, words separated by spaces, in the order used by `scripts/emblem_tests2.py`. Sampled from the all-titles-in-ns0 dumps (dumps.wikimedia.org, fetched 29 October 2026). Content: Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0. The EDH Latin inscriptions are fetched by `scripts/fetch_data.sh` (CC BY-SA 4.0).
